## 6. Rental Policies & Requirements

### Driver Age & Licence:
- **Minimum Age**: 21 years old. Drivers aged 21-24 are subject to a standard young driver surcharge of ₹15/day.
- **Licence Requirement**: Full, unrestricted driver's licence held for a minimum of 12 continuous months.
- **International Visitors**: English licence or valid International Driving Permit (IDP) accompanied by home country licence and passport.

### Fuel Policy:
- Full-to-Full policy on all passenger vehicles. Return with a full tank to avoid a refuelling charge of ₹3.50/L plus service fee.

### Payment & Security Deposit:
- Security bond of ₹200 (or ₹0 when Zero Excess Protection is purchased) pre-authorised on credit/debit card at pickup.
- Online payments are processed securely via Razorpay gateway supporting Visa, MasterCard, AMEX, and UPI.

### Cancellation & Modifications:
- **Free Cancellation**: Up to 48 hours prior to scheduled pickup time with a 100% full refund.
- **Late Cancellation (under 48 hours)**: Subject to a 1-day rental fee retention.
- **No-Show**: Non-refundable.

## 7. FAQs & Customer Assistance

### Q1: Can I drive interstate?
Yes, NR Car Hire allows travel across state borders throughout mainland Australia. One-way drop-offs between major airport hubs are supported.

### Q2: What happens if I return the vehicle late?
A 59-minute grace period is provided. Returns exceeding 1 hour are charged at the standard daily rate.

### Q3: Are vehicles allowed on unsealed roads?
Standard passenger vehicles (Sedan, SUV, Luxury) must remain on sealed public bitumen roads. The Toyota HiLux 4x4 may travel on graded unsealed roads.
